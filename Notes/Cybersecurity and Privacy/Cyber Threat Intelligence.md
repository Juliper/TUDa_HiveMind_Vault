**Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)** is the process that transforms data into actionable, evidence-based insights, allowing security teams to detect, prevent, and respond to attacks rather than merely react.

## Data Pipeline
Raw data from the operational environment is collected, processed, and analyzed. Only at the end of this pipeline do actionable insights (intelligence) emerge.
